# Fonts bundled with Cameo

The interface uses original, unmodified font files distributed by the Google Fonts project. These are openly licensed fonts, not Apple fonts. The generated concept PNGs are picker previews; native text and controls are rendered separately.

- **Inter**, by the Inter Project Authors. Font file: `fonts/Inter-Variable.ttf`. Official source: https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/inter . Downloaded from https://raw.githubusercontent.com/google/fonts/main/ofl/inter/Inter%5Bopsz%2Cwght%5D.ttf . Full original SIL Open Font License and copyright notice: `fonts/Inter-OFL.txt`.
- **Cormorant Garamond**, by the Cormorant Project Authors. Font file: `fonts/CormorantGaramond-Variable.ttf`. Official source: https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/cormorantgaramond . Downloaded from https://raw.githubusercontent.com/google/fonts/main/ofl/cormorantgaramond/CormorantGaramond%5Bwght%5D.ttf . Full original SIL Open Font License and copyright notice: `fonts/CormorantGaramond-OFL.txt`.
- **Instrument Serif**, copyright 2022 The Instrument Serif Project Authors. Font file: `fonts/InstrumentSerif-Regular.ttf`. Official source: https://github.com/Instrument/instrument-serif . Google Fonts distribution: https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/instrumentserif . Full original SIL Open Font License and copyright notice: `fonts/InstrumentSerif-OFL.txt`.

All three font families are distributed under the SIL Open Font License, Version 1.1. Copies of their respective original licenses are included alongside the font files. No font is sold separately or renamed. The historical television Premiere renderer uses Cormorant Garamond for its feature title; this renderer is retained internally, but its theme is no longer selectable. The mobile Pearl theme uses Instrument Serif for its brand, feature titles and section headings. Other current native theme text uses Inter, with selected platform font styles for condensed cinematic titles. A platform font fallback is available if loading a bundled font fails.
