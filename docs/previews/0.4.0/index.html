These are controlled native test renders, not physical Z Fold 8 or Sony TV screenshots. They are rendered from Android views by Robolectric at fixed canvas sizes. The source concepts remain unchanged and are shown separately.

The layouts use real film titles and catalog artwork. The original studies use fictional films, different artwork and different title lengths. System status and gesture bars are outside these app-canvas renders. Typography and software-rendered image clipping also differ; a pixel-for-pixel match has not been verified.

Mobile appearances

Glass uses a 390 × 844 canvas; the other four use 390 × 891. These are provisional logical viewports. The Z Fold 8 cover and unfolded layouts also have geometry checks at representative sizes; physical phone testing remains separate.

Television appearances

Each native layout uses a 960 × 540 canvas, with a feature area, three initial Continue Watching cards and five initial Discover cards. Additional titles scroll horizontally; account-specific Up Next episodes continue below the first viewport.

What this review establishes: the native controls, live artwork composition, theme colors and initial browsing geometry can be inspected without exposing private accounts. It does not establish physical-device playback, receiver compatibility or exact visual equivalence.
